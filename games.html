Overview

General

As of July 20, 2026 there are like 10 games on vortex, the most notable and popular being: House, Vortexia, Demo, Oasis, Backrooms and the rest are largely irrelevant.

Demo

Demo has existed since April 2026, It was the first game to feature shirts and plants, the entire map is a colorful Obby that many users found difficult. for a brief period you could walk out the map but now you just fall into the void. Halo and others used this map as testing for various mechanics and physics. You can climb and parkour on that map. It usually takes a ~60 seconds to parkour to the top for fast speedrunners.

House

House has existed since April 2026, It was the most popular Vortex game of April and May 2026, with the most amount of chaos and bugs happening here, the entire map was a red roblox house on a green baseplate. for a brief period you could walk out the map but now you just fall into the void. Halo and others used this map for greeting players, while a lot of players hacked, talked or became AFK for hours. You can climb and get on the roof on the map. The Map was revamped and no longer had a large baseplate, it became a island with tree's. Many raiders and long-time veterans remember this map.

Banlands

Banlands has a lot of glitches and it's spooky and famous for cults and creepypastas, other than that, not many players play it anymore.

Pirate Ship

Pirate Ship is a map with a pirate ship, some water and a island with a palm tree. mostly used for hangouts, it's largely irrelevant.

Backrooms

The backrooms were a popular map at first, it's just the backrooms, mainly used for glitching and hide and seek, you can get lost easily or do parkours.

Early History (April — June 2026)

Games used to be on the website in the browser instead of a dedicated app, anyone could just hit play and join without downloading anything. in May 2026 there was only 2 vortex games, Demo and House. This later changed when more games were added and the website games were discontinued. Then new games appeared.

New History (July — August 2026)

By July 2026 a vortex game reached 100 CCU, by Early August Vortex Studio released and users started making their own games but at first weren't publishable.
